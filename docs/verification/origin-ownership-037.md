## Remaining acceptance

These proofs use controlled transports, happy-dom storage events, isolated HTTP/fake engines and named browser components. They do not close persisted two-client invitations/revocation, native Convex subscriptions/restart persistence, full-App private-cache eviction or all of [Batch A](origin-feature-acceptance-034.md#finite-unresolved-acceptance). Local epochs are not durable distributed fencing or atomic shared-storage transactions. Interrupting a run cannot undo an external action already dispatched, and exactly-once external effects are not established. No real recipient email, production mutation, packaged GUI/updater, physical Mac/hardware or TURN acceptance occurred. Central email readiness keeps its original 031 time; provider acceptance/inbox delivery remain unverified. The full inspection and daily 06:00 Europe/Rome review continue.
